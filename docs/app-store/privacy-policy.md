# StarSync Privacy Policy

<!-- Published at https://chipcressman.com/starsync/privacy, from starsync/privacy.html in the Portfolio repo. Change both together. -->

*Last updated 17 November 2026*

StarSync collects no data about you.

**Nothing leaves your Mac.** StarSync makes no network connections. It has no
account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it shares
nothing with anyone — including the developer.

**What it reads.** The Lightroom library you choose, opened read-only, and the
photos in your Photos library, which macOS asks your permission for first.
Ratings, file names, capture dates and image sizes are read so that each
Lightroom photo can be matched with its copy in Photos. That happens on your
Mac, in memory.

**What it writes.** Star ratings, to the photos in Photos you've reviewed and
approved. Nothing else about any photo is changed, and your Lightroom library
is never written to.

**What it keeps.** A record of each sync — which photos it changed, and their
ratings before and after — so that Revert can undo it. It stays inside the
app's own container on your Mac, and deleting the app deletes it.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: hello@chipcressman.com.
