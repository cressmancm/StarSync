StarSync Review Kit
===================

StarSync copies the star ratings you give photos in Adobe Lightroom into
Apple Photos. This kit lets you try it without Lightroom.

  Demo Photos            Eight photos, DEMO_0001 to DEMO_0008, unrated.
  Demo Library.lrlibrary A Lightroom library that rates seven of them,
                         from one star to five, and leaves DEMO_0007 unrated.


Trying it
---------

1. Import the photos into Photos: drag the Demo Photos folder onto the
   Photos window, or use File > Import.

2. Open StarSync and allow access to Photos when asked. It needs to read
   and write, because writing star ratings is what it does.

3. Click "Choose Lightroom Library..." and choose Demo Library.lrlibrary
   from this folder.

4. Choose the "StarSync Review" album, then click Continue.

5. Review shows 7 ratings to write, and DEMO_0007 under "Leaving alone":
   it is unrated in Lightroom, so StarSync skips it. Nothing has been
   written yet.

6. Click "Write 7 ratings". In Photos, select DEMO_0001 and open the Info
   panel (Command-I): it now has five stars.

7. Back in StarSync, click "Revert this sync". The ratings are removed
   from Photos again.


To see a conflict
-----------------

Before step 4, give DEMO_0002 one star in Photos. Review will then ask you
to choose between Lightroom's four stars and Photos' one, and writes
nothing for that photo until you do.


StarSync opens the Lightroom library read-only and doesn't change it. The
only thing it writes to Photos is the star rating. It makes no network
connections.
